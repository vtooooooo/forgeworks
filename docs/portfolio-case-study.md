# Portfolio case study

ForgeWorks uses time-phased material accounting and machine sequencing to establish deterministic feasibility. A separate digital twin evaluates feasible plans under common disturbance streams. The copilot exposes checked operational records so a planner can compare alternatives and approve an auditable candidate.

In the published benchmark, simulation selected a buffered ML schedule with lower mean weighted tardiness and fewer shifted starts than the unbuffered ML schedule. The unbuffered schedule delivered a larger fraction on time and cost less in the synthetic model. The extra Saturday shift in the full demonstration left outcomes unchanged, while the breakdown scenario affected the released plan and prompted a protected replan.

Measured results, sample counts, uncertainty intervals and reproducibility instructions are in the [delivery report](../reports/final-delivery.md).
