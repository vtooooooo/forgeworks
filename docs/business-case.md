# Business case

ForgeWorks Manufacturing is a fictional US precision metal components producer. The planner commits machines and stock to orders moving through cutting, machining and finishing. Uncertain cycle times, breakdowns, late replenishment and expedited orders create delivery risk.

A constraint check answers whether a schedule can execute under its nominal inputs. A solver bound measures the objective's quality for that mathematical model. A forecast estimates operation duration in minutes. A simulated probability measures behavior under a specified execution policy and disturbance model. Plant performance requires actual production evidence and field validation. Planners can review those questions independently before making a commitment.
